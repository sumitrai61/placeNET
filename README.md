# Placement Management System  

A web-based platform designed to simplify and streamline the campus placement process.  
It bridges the gap between **students**, **placement coordinators**, and **recruiters** by providing past stats, real experiences, and tools to manage placement drives.  

---

## 👥 Users
- **Students** – Explore placement stats, interview experiences, and reviews from seniors.  
- **Placement Coordinators (PCs)** – Manage company contacts, organize drives, and track placement activities.  
- **Recruiters** – Connect with coordinators and access streamlined placement logistics.  

---

## 🚀 Features
- **Past Placement Stats** – Access historical data on company-wise placements, salary trends, and more.  
- **Students’ Experiences** – Learn from the real interview experiences shared by placed students.  
- **Reviews from Seniors** – Honest feedback on company culture, rounds, and preparation.  
- **Upcoming Company Insights** – Click on a company to see expected questions, past test patterns, and senior insights.  
- **Coordinator Dashboard** – Keep track of HR contacts, placement schedules, and drive organization.  
- **Drive Management** – End-to-end assistance for smooth placement drives.  

---

## 🎯 Purpose
This project aims to:  
- Help **students** prepare better for upcoming drives.  
- Assist **coordinators** in managing the placement process efficiently.  
- Provide **recruiters** a structured way to engage with campuses.  

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack (suggested)
- **Frontend** – HTML, CSS, Bootstrap  
- **Backend** – PHP  
- **Database** – MySQL
